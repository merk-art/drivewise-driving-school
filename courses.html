Our Driving Courses

Whether you are getting behind the wheel for the first time or looking to improve your driving skills, DriveWise has a training program to suit your needs.

Our courses combine theory, practical driving and road safety training to help learners become confident and responsible drivers.

Beginner Driving Course

This course is designed for learners who are completely new to driving. We take you through the basics step by step.

What You Will Learn

  • Basic vehicle controls
  • Starting and stopping the vehicle
  • Road signs and markings
  • Traffic rules
  • Basic road driving
  • Parking and manoeuvring
  • Road safety

Duration: 30 Lessons

Sample Price: KSh 15,000

Manual Driving Course

This course focuses on learning how to operate a manual transmission vehicle safely and confidently.

What You Will Learn

  • Clutch control
  • Gear changing
  • Starting and stopping
  • Hill starts
  • Parking
  • Turning and changing lanes
  • Practical road driving

Duration: 20 Lessons

Sample Price: KSh 12,000

Automatic Driving Course

Learn how to confidently operate an automatic vehicle while developing safe and responsible driving habits.

What You Will Learn

  • Automatic vehicle controls
  • Acceleration and braking
  • Steering control
  • Parking
  • Traffic management
  • Road positioning
  • Practical road training

Duration: 20 Lessons

Sample Price: KSh 14,000

Refresher Driving Course

This course is designed for drivers who already know how to drive but want to improve their confidence and practical driving skills.

What You Will Learn

  • Improving road confidence
  • City and busy-road driving
  • Parking practice
  • Traffic awareness
  • Road positioning
  • Defensive driving techniques

Duration: 10 Lessons

Sample Price: KSh 7,000

Defensive Driving Course

This course focuses on helping drivers recognise hazards, make safer decisions and respond appropriately to different road situations.

What You Will Learn

  • Hazard awareness
  • Safe following distances
  • Understanding road risks
  • Emergency situations
  • Defensive driving techniques
  • Responsible road behaviour

Duration: 5 Lessons

Sample Price: KSh 5,000
