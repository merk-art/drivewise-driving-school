Who We Are

DriveWise Driving School is a modern driving school focused on providing quality and practical driving education. We believe that learning to drive is more than simply operating a vehicle. It is about understanding the road, making responsible decisions and developing confidence behind the wheel.

Choose the Right Course for You

Whether you are getting behind the wheel for the first time or looking to improve your driving skills, DriveWise has a training program to suit your needs.

Beginner Driving

Start your driving journey with our beginner course. Learn vehicle controls, road signs, traffic rules, basic manoeuvres and safe road driving.

Duration: 30 Lessons

Price: KSh 15,000

Manual Driving

Master clutch control, gear changing, hill starts, parking and confident manual driving.

Duration: 20 Lessons

Price: KSh 12,000

Automatic Driving

Learn to drive an automatic vehicle with a focus on road awareness, vehicle control, parking and confidence.

Duration: 20 Lessons

Price: KSh 14,000

Refresher Driving

Already know how to drive but need more confidence? Our refresher course helps you improve your skills and become more comfortable on the road.

Duration: 10 Lessons

Price: KSh 7,000
