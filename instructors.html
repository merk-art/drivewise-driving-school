Our Driving Instructors

At DriveWise Driving School, we have experienced and professional instructors who are committed to helping learners become safe, confident and responsible drivers.

Our instructors use practical training methods and patient guidance to make learning how to drive easier and more enjoyable.

Our Teaching Approach

We believe that every learner is different. Our instructors therefore take time to understand each learner's abilities and areas that require improvement.

  1. Introduction to vehicle controls
  2. Basic vehicle movement
  3. Road and traffic rules
  4. Practical road driving
  5. Parking and manoeuvring
  6. Defensive driving techniques
